# Match score (0-100), editable

| Area | Points | What earns them |
|---|---|---|
| Required qualifications met | 35 | Share of must-haves the user truly meets, with evidence on the resume |
| Keywords and tools present | 20 | Posting terms the user really has, shown in context (not only in a skills list) |
| Seniority and scope fit | 15 | Team size, quota, revenue, territory, level compared to the posting |
| Quantified results | 15 | Real numbers relevant to the role |
| ATS format and clarity | 10 | Clean structure, standard headings, readable text extraction |
| Preferred qualifications | 5 | Nice-to-haves met |

Gate: 70 or higher to apply, 90 or higher is the optimization target. Never raise a score by adding unconfirmed facts. Judge fit on whether requirements are met; a role at a reputable company with upside is not "below level" just because of title.
