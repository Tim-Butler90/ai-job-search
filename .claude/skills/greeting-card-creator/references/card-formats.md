# Card formats and print setup

Defaults are editable. Confirm with the user's printer if they use one; printer specs override these numbers.

| Folded size | Flat sheet (open) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 5 x 7 in (A7-ish common) | 10 x 7 in | Most common; fits an envelope A7 (5.25 x 7.25 in) |
| 4.25 x 5.5 in | 8.5 x 5.5 in | Quarter of a US Letter sheet; easy home printing |
| 4 x 6 in | 8 x 6 in | Fits 4x6 photo paper |
| A6 105 x 148 mm | 210 x 148 mm | Common in Europe |
| A5 148 x 210 mm | 296 x 210 mm | Large card |

Home printing option: place a 5x7 folded card as a two-panel layout on landscape US Letter (11 x 8.5 in); the flat 10 x 7 area fits with 0.5 in margins. For a quarter-fold card, use a portrait Letter sheet divided into four panels (front, back, and two inside panels, with the front and inside printed rotated as needed).

## Single-fold (side-fold) layout
Flat sheet is two panels side by side, fold at the center.
- Outside sheet (print side 1): left panel = back, right panel = front.
- Inside sheet (print side 2): left panel = inside left, right panel = inside right.
For one-sided home printing, print the outside and inside on separate sheets, or use a single page showing both for the user to cut and paste; state which method is used.

## Print specs
- Bleed: 0.125 in (3 mm) beyond trim on all sides if art touches the edge.
- Safe margin: keep text and key art at least 0.25 in inside the trim, and 0.25 in from the fold line.
- Resolution: 300 ppi for images at final size.
- Paper: 80-110 lb cover (216-300 gsm) folds cleanly; score the fold for heavy stock.
- Fonts: embed or outline in PDFs; minimum 9 pt for body text, larger for older recipients.

## Panel content guide
- Front: greeting, or art plus a short line.
- Inside left: art, blank, or a short secondary line.
- Inside right: the message and sign-off.
- Back: small credit or a short note; keep it quiet.
