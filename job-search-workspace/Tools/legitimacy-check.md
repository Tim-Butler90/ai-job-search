# Legitimacy and ghost-job check
Mark "likely stale" and rank last if any apply:
- Posted 60+ days ago, or reposted 2+ times.
- No team, manager, or pay range named; vague duties; evergreen "pipeline" wording.
- Posting exists only on a job board, not the employer's own site/ATS.
- Employer domain, ATS and company details do not match; asks for money, SSN, or off-platform chat.
- Role closed or redirects when the apply link is opened.
Always confirm the apply button works on the employer's own page before listing a lead.
